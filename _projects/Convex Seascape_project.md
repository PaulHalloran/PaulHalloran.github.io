The Convex Seascape Survey is an insurance industry funded project (Convex), dedicated to filling knowledge gaps cean seabed carbon storage and the impact of seabed disturbance on released CO2. Halloran, Lenton and Arellano Nava are undertaking teh development and aplication of a new coupled water-column/seabed biogeochemical model to explore the impacts of seabed disturbance.
